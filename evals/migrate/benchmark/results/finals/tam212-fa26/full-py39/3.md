## Calendars and schedule decisions

I used the university’s official [Spring 2026 calendar](https://senate.illinois.edu/ep/Props/1718/Final/ep1844.final.pdf) and [Fall 2026 calendar](https://senate.illinois.edu/ep/Props/1718/Final/ep1845.final.pdf). Spring instruction runs January 20–May 6, with spring break March 14–22 and finals May 8–15. Fall instruction runs August 24–December 9, with Labor Day September 7, fall break November 21–29, and finals December 11–18.

The main patterns are weekly discussions, Monday homework releases with staged deadlines, and practice quizzes every other week. I applied these defaults:

- Moved the homework deadline that landed on Labor Day to **Tuesday, September 8**. Five releases remain on Labor Day; releases on holidays are allowed by the workflow default.
- Tied the six practice quizzes with leftover 2024 dates to the Fall quiz sequence: September 8, September 21, October 5, October 19, November 2, and November 16. Their windows close on December 18.
- Kept dates aligned by instructional week across breaks. This means some windows change length: `hw04a` and `hw04b` are 14 days rather than 21; `hw06b` and `hw07` span fall break and are 21 days rather than 14; `DS12` also spans fall break.
- Removed 48 legacy per-student UID rules. The scan showed one 13-person group repeated across 27 assessments, one six-person group across six practice quizzes, and several smaller groups. **`hwtracker` is now staff-only** because it had no general access rule left. Recreate any staff, CA, or accommodation access that should continue.
- Kept the old PrairieTest exam rules in the copy. Their reservations and UUIDs need to be replaced in PrairieTest for Fall 2026.
- Left shared questions untouched. The copy retains the legacy `allowAccess` format; PrairieLearn’s Access tabs can migrate it later.

The scan found **no remaining out-of-term dates or deadlines on holidays or during breaks**. It found five releases on Labor Day, as noted above. I did not verify a PrairieLearn sync.
