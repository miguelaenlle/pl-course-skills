Problem statement

Why the statistics page isn’t enough

One term can mislead

Terrain scored 33 points below Temperature one spring and above it the next fall. Each term served a different alternative on different exam days.

Scores hide workload

Two variants can average the same while one takes 4 more minutes and 3 more attempts.

The data has traps

Blanket credit, best-2-of-3 zones, retakes with five students, and scores bumped by staff all distort the numbers.

No view of the failing step

A 40% score doesn’t say which part students missed. Per-test grader results do, but nothing shows them.

These four come straight from the CS 357 and TAM 212 analyses. The first one is the most dangerous: an instructor reading one term would swap out the wrong question.
