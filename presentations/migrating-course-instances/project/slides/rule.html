The fall dates are what CS 357’s instructor chose by hand. The rule reproduces them.

Weeks are counted from the week instruction begins, skipping any week that is entirely break (UIUC registrar calendars: spring break Mar 14 to 22, 2026; fall break Nov 21 to 29, 2026). Dates before instruction, in finals, or after the term keep their offset from that boundary. A deadline that spanned spring break spans one fewer calendar week in fall, as here. The script flags dates that land on holidays or in breaks, come out of order, shrink their window by more than half, or are already past.
